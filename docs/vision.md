# Vision Statement 

The Story Machine is a platform where anyone can write a story in which the reader decides what happens next, publish it, and read stories written by others, with the purpose of bringing the authors and readers together in a community where they can share feedback and comments on stories.

An author who wants to write a story where the reader makes the choices often has to pick between a tool that understands choices and a community that has readers. Existing story builders can handle these stories but usually leave authors responsible for distributing their work, while general story-sharing communities provide an audience but are designed for stories read from start to finish. On those sites, authors are left linking chapters by hand, which makes stories difficult to maintain as they change. The Story Machine offers both in one place. Authors can write their story naturally, mark the moments where the reader can make decisions, decide how the story looks and sounds, and publish it directly to a community of readers. Readers can browse for stories that interest them, make choices that shape the ending, continue later from where they left off, follow the authors they enjoy, and comment on a story they read.

The Story Machine is designed for people who love stories, whether they want to write them or read them. For authors, this includes people writing their first interactive story as well as experienced writers who want to create stories with complex branching paths and multiple endings. They should be able to focus on writing and making creative decisions without needing to understand how to build or maintain the underlying structure of an interactive story or having to find and develop an audience separately. For readers, this means people who want more than simply reading from beginning to end. They can discover stories based on their interests, influence what happens through their choices, and share their reactions with the author and with others who read the same story.

The Story Machine's success will be measured by growth, readership, and sustained engagement between its authors and readers. Our primary goals are to reach 1,000 monthly active users, including at least 100 authors with a published story, and to maintain both for three consecutive months. Once at least 100 stories have been published, we aim for at least 80% of published stories to be read by five or more people within a week of publishing. Once at least 250 readers are active, we aim for at least 70% of readers who begin a story to reach one of its endings and at least 50% of readers to return to read another story within a month. These measures will show that our platform is not only functional but valued by its community.
